# DUUI-Py Evaluation Presentation

Open `duui_py_evaluation_presentation.ipynb` for the professor-facing notebook.

Current structure: motivation, system overview, events/telemetry, codec and adapter design, evaluation setup, results for spaCy/TaxoNERD/Gazetteer, GNFinder evidence limit, bottlenecks, limitations, conclusion, references, and provenance.

Terminology is standardized to `baseline` and `async variant`; raw artifact paths are kept only where needed for provenance. Figures are embedded with explicit scaled widths in the notebook.
